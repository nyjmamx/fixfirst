Privacy & data transparency

What stays on your device

Diagnosis history is stored in this browser's local storage. It is not synced to an account. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to view it.

When data leaves FIXFIRST

Choosing Google Maps opens a third-party service and shares the search terms or coordinates with Google. Location is requested only when you tap “Find shops near me.”

How to delete history

Use the “Clear history” button in the recent reports panel. Browser storage may also be cleared in browser settings.

What we do not offer yet

No user accounts, payment collection, live AI scanning, or verified shop partnerships are included in this beta.

Hosting providers may process standard technical request logs when the site loads. We have not independently audited the host's logging practices. Avoid entering sensitive personal information.

Questions before you begin?

Is FIXFIRST free?

Yes, this beta is free. No payment information or activation key is required.

Can FIXFIRST guarantee what is broken?

No. Results narrow down possible causes based on your answers. They are educational guidance, not a professional inspection.

Will FIXFIRST access my phone or computer files?

No. This beta does not scan your device or request access to personal files.

Are repair shops vetted by FIXFIRST?

No. The repair search opens Google Maps. Check independent reviews, pricing, credentials, and warranty terms before booking.

What if I am not comfortable with a repair step?

Skip it. Stop if there is a safety concern and consult a qualified repair professional.
